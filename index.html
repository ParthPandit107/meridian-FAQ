Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are your clinic hours?

A: Meridian Health Clinic is open Monday–Saturday, 8am–7pm. Our Downtown location is also open Sundays, 9am–2pm.

Q: Where are your locations?

A: We have three locations: Downtown (123 Main St), Westside (45 Oak Ave), and Eastgate (890 Pine Rd).

Q: Do you accept my insurance?

A: We accept most major PPO insurance plans. We currently do not accept HMO plans. Bring your insurance card to your first visit so we can verify coverage.

Q: What should I bring to my first appointment?

A: Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of any current medications. New patients should also arrive 15 minutes early to complete an intake form.

Q: Do you offer dental services?

A: Yes, our Westside and Downtown locations offer general and cosmetic dentistry, including cleanings, fillings, and checkups.

Q: Do you offer mental health counseling?

A: Yes, our Eastgate location has licensed counselors offering individual therapy sessions. Appointments can be booked directly or via referral.

Q: Can I get lab work done?

A: Yes, all three locations have on-site diagnostic labs for bloodwork and basic imaging.

Q: How do I book an appointment?

A: You can book directly through this chat — I just need a few details from you.

Q: What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

A: Please give us at least 24 hours' notice by calling your clinic location directly.

Q: Do you treat children?

A: Yes, our Downtown and Westside locations accept patients of all ages, including pediatric care.

Q: How much does a visit cost without insurance?

A: Self-pay visits start at $120 for a standard consultation. Costs vary by service — our front desk can give you an exact quote for your specific visit type.

Q: Is parking available?

A: Yes, all three locations have free on-site patient parking.

Q: Do you offer telehealth / video appointments?

A: Yes, for follow-up visits and mental health counseling sessions, we offer video appointments. First-time visits are generally in-person.

Q: What age groups do you treat?

A: We treat patients of all ages at our Downtown and Westside locations. Eastgate primarily serves adult patients for counseling and diagnostics.

Q: How early should I arrive for my appointment?

A: New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake paperwork. Returning patients can arrive right at their appointment time.

Q: Can I get a prescription refill through this chat?

A: I can't process prescription refills directly, but I can help you book a follow-up appointment or connect you with the right team for a refill request.

Q: Do you have weekend appointments?

A: Our Downtown location is open Sundays 9am–2pm. Westside and Eastgate are Monday–Saturday only.

Q: What's the difference between your locations?

A: Downtown offers general medicine, dental, and diagnostics. Westside focuses on general and dental care. Eastgate specializes in mental health counseling and diagnostics.

Q: Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

A: For most services, no referral is needed — you can book directly. Some specialist services may require a referral from your primary provider; I can check this for you if you tell me what you're looking to book.

Q: Is my information kept private?

A: Yes, all patient information is handled confidentially in line with standard healthcare privacy practices.
