Frequently Asked Questions

01 What are your clinic hours?

Meridian Health Clinic is open Monday–Saturday, 8am–7pm. Our Downtown location is also open Sundays, 9am–2pm.

02 Do you have weekend appointments?

Our Downtown location is open Sundays 9am–2pm. Westside and Eastgate are Monday–Saturday only.

03 How early should I arrive for my appointment?

New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake paperwork. Returning patients can arrive right at their appointment time.

04 Where are your locations?

We have three locations: Downtown (123 Main St), Westside (45 Oak Ave), and Eastgate (890 Pine Rd).

05 What's the difference between your locations?

Downtown offers general medicine, dental, and diagnostics. Westside focuses on general and dental care. Eastgate specializes in mental health counseling and diagnostics.

06 Do you offer dental services?

Yes, our Westside and Downtown locations offer general and cosmetic dentistry, including cleanings, fillings, and checkups.

07 Do you offer mental health counseling?

Yes, our Eastgate location has licensed counselors offering individual therapy sessions. Appointments can be booked directly or via referral.

08 Can I get lab work done?

Yes, all three locations have on-site diagnostic labs for bloodwork and basic imaging.

09 Do you treat children?

Yes, our Downtown and Westside locations accept patients of all ages, including pediatric care.

10 What age groups do you treat?

We treat patients of all ages at our Downtown and Westside locations. Eastgate primarily serves adult patients for counseling and diagnostics.

11 Is parking available?

Yes, all three locations have free on-site patient parking.

12 How do I book an appointment?

You can book directly through this chat — I just need a few details from you.

13 What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice by calling your clinic location directly.

14 What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of any current medications. New patients should also arrive 15 minutes early to complete an intake form.

15 Do you offer telehealth / video appointments?

Yes, for follow-up visits and mental health counseling sessions, we offer video appointments. First-time visits are generally in-person.

16 Can I get a prescription refill through this chat?

I can't process prescription refills directly, but I can help you book a follow-up appointment or connect you with the right team for a refill request.

17 Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

For most services, no referral is needed — you can book directly. Some specialist services may require a referral from your primary provider; I can check this for you if you tell me what you're looking to book.

18 Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major PPO insurance plans. We currently do not accept HMO plans. Bring your insurance card to your first visit so we can verify coverage.

19 How much does a visit cost without insurance?

Self-pay visits start at $120 for a standard consultation. Costs vary by service — our front desk can give you an exact quote for your specific visit type.

20 Is my information kept private?

Yes, all patient information is handled confidentially in line with standard healthcare privacy practices.
